# Turn taking, barge-in and echo (LiveKit pipeline, simulated sound card)

Generated by `voice/turn_eval.py`: the production session (`agent_cascaded.build_session`) on LiveKit's console audio classes, with the sound card simulated in real time (100 ms blocks, 24 kHz, the console's WebRTC audio processing). Speech is synthetic (Deepgram Aura); LiveKit 1.8.3 defaults: endpointing 0.5-3.0 s, VAD minimum silence 0.55 s, AEC warm-up 3 s.

## 2. Barge-in (the user interrupts the agent 4 s into an answer)

- Interrupted: **12 of 12** answers.
- **Stop latency** (the user's speech onset -> the agent's audio paused, i.e. the sound stops): **p50 616 ms, p95 629 ms** (+ up to 100 ms for the block already playing).
- The answer is dropped (audio cleared) once the interruption is confirmed: p50 2,291 ms, p95 2,329 ms; until then LiveKit would resume a false interruption (`resume_false_interruption`, 2 s).
- Inside the AEC warm-up (the user speaks 1.5 s into the session's first answer): audio paused only after 1,510 ms, when the 3 s warm-up ended; answer not dropped: the agent carried on.
